## CodeCompose

CodeCompose builds productivity apps powered by AI and offers courses on deep learning, programming, and developer culture.

### What we do

**Apps**: We create thoughtful applications that help people focus, learn, and work more effectively. Our flagship product, FlowState AI, uses on-device AI to measure real concentration levels — a first in the productivity app space.

**Education**: We offer customized courses for teams, schools, and study groups on topics including deep learning, Kubernetes, mobile/web development, and software engineering workflows. Visit [courses.codecompose.net](https://courses.codecompose.net/en/) for more details.

### Our apps

- **[FlowState AI](/en/apps/flowstate-ai/)** — AI-powered focus timer with real-time concentration tracking (iOS & Android)
- **[Focus Capsule](/en/apps/focus-capsule/)** — Simple Pomodoro timer for the Apple ecosystem (iOS, iPad, Mac, Apple Watch)
